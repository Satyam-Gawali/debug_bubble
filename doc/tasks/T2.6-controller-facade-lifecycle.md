- Stale controller isolation:
  - References to disposed/replaced controllers are inert: logging does not throw, does not modify active store, and does not notify listeners.
  - Getters and operations on disposed controllers return empty/zero values and never throw (L-8).
- Listener & disposal safety:
  - `dispose()` is idempotent and notifies listeners once before disposing ChangeNotifier (L-8).
  - Calling `addListener()` or `removeListener()` after controller disposal does not throw.
- Sequence IDs & monotonic continuity:
  - Sequence IDs increment monotonically starting at 1 per controller.
  - Clearing a controller empties entries and resets `droppedCount`, while preserving sequence ID monotonicity.
  - Creating a new default controller starts a fresh sequence at 1.
